Nothing in your layout is wrong on its own. It feels busy because borders, brackets, emoji and repeated text all have the same visual weight, so nothing stands out. Here's what I'd change, most important first.

## What's making it noisy

**1. Three levels of nested boxes.** You have an outer frame, a frame per pane, and another box inside each pane. Every level costs 2–4 columns plus a row on top and bottom, so most of what you see is lines, and that's why it feels cramped at 120 columns. Your mockup is only about 79 columns wide, and roughly 12 of those are border on each side. Use one level of borders and let whitespace do the rest.

**2. The selection indicator is on every row.** `▶` appears on all five pipelines, so you can't tell which one is selected. This is a usability bug, not just a looks problem. Show the cursor only on the selected row, or better, highlight that row with a background color.

**3. Status is shown twice, in noisy ways.** `[PASS]`, `[FAIL]`, `[....]` and `[WAIT]` are four-letter codes in brackets, which you have to read rather than glance at. `✅ PASSED ✅` shows the same status three times. A colored symbol does the same job in one cell: `✓ ✗ ⠋ ○`.

**4. The emoji are breaking your alignment.** Look at the right edges of your mockup: several lines are off by one. `✅` is 2 cells wide and terminals don't agree on how to render it. Use single-width symbols and measure strings with `lipgloss.Width()`, not `len()`.

**5. Log lines repeat themselves.** Every line has `[INFO]`, the full date and `:: … ::` decoration. The date is the same on every line, and INFO is the default, so neither tells you anything. Show the time only, and show the level only when it's WARN or ERROR (in color).

**6. The space goes to the wrong things.** Logs are what you actually read when something fails, but they get 3 rows. The details pane takes half the width for five short fields.

**7. The chrome isn't earning its space.** `v2.1` in the title and brackets around every key in the footer add clutter. Use the title bar for useful context like the repo and when data was last refreshed.

## What I'd do instead

Put the list on the left. On the right, a single pane shows the selected pipeline: details collapsed into one line, then logs filling the rest of the height.

What changed:
- **One border level.** Rounded borders look softer than square ones.
- **The pane title is the selected job.** That replaces the separate "Details" box and the `Job:` row.
- **Duration is in the list**, so you can compare pipelines without selecting each one.
- **Logs get all the remaining height.** At 120 columns and 40 rows, that's around 30 lines instead of 3.
- **Color carries most of the meaning.** That's why it can lose the brackets, emoji, labels and borders.

## Hierarchy with Lipgloss instead of boxes

- **Focus:** show which pane is active by its border color (`BorderForeground`, bright for active, dim gray for inactive). Don't add more chrome for it.
- **Status colors:** green ✓, red ✗, yellow spinner (`bubbles/spinner`), and dim ○ for pending. A failed pipeline should be the brightest thing on screen.
- **De-emphasize** timestamps, the footer, metadata separators (`·`) and the refresh time with `Faint(true)` or a mid-gray. Only the job names and log text should be full brightness.
- **Selected row:** a subtle background color on the whole row reads better than `▸` alone. Keep `▸` as well if you want it to work without color.
- **Sizing:** calculate widths from `tea.WindowSizeMsg`. Fix the list at about 30 columns and give the rest to logs. Use `lipgloss.JoinHorizontal` and `bubbles/viewport` for the scrolling log.
- **Footer:** show only 4–5 keys and put the rest behind `?` (`bubbles/help` handles this). `enter` doesn't need a hint if moving the cursor already updates the right pane.

## Optional extras

- Sort or group failed pipelines to the top.
- Add a filter to show only failures.
- Auto-scroll the log to the first ERROR line when you select a failed job.
