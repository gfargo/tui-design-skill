codex
No—`RestoreTerminal()` means **restore Bubble Tea’s control of the terminal after `ReleaseTerminal()`**. It can re-enable raw mode and restart rendering/input. Calling it after `Run()` can undo the cleanup and leave your terminal broken.

Use:

```go
p := tea.NewProgram(model)
_, err := p.Run()
return err
```

Bubble Tea handles terminal cleanup when `Run()` finishes and includes panic recovery by default. Keep that recovery enabled; don’t use `tea.WithoutCatchPanics()` if you want its protection.

For other cases:

- **Temporarily hand the terminal to another process:** use `tea.ExecProcess`, or pair `ReleaseTerminal()` with `RestoreTerminal()`.
- **Request normal shutdown:** return `tea.Quit` from your model, or call `p.Quit()` externally.
- **Force shutdown:** call `p.Kill()`.

Panic recovery is scoped to goroutines. A panic in your own background goroutine needs recovery there; a defer around `Run()` cannot catch it. Also avoid `os.Exit` or `log.Fatal` while the program is running, since they bypass deferred cleanup.
