I'm building a TUI for browsing Kubernetes pods: a namespace sidebar, a pod list, and a detail pane with logs. Design the layout for me.

codex
Use a three-pane layout with **namespaces on the left, pods in the center, and pod details plus logs on the right**. Give logs the most space.

- **Sizing:** Namespace sidebar 20–24 columns; pod list roughly 35% of remaining width; details take the rest. Use subtle dividers and a clear border or title accent around the focused pane.
- **Pod list:** Default columns are name, readiness, and age. Show unhealthy status beneath the affected pod so long names remain readable. Add status and restart columns when space permits.
- **Details:** Keep a compact metadata block above tabs for **Logs, Events, and YAML**. Make container selection explicit for multi-container pods.
- **Logs:** Follow automatically when first opened. Scrolling upward pauses following and shows **“Paused · f to resume.”** Keep timestamps muted; reserve strong color for warnings and errors.
- **Navigation:** `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` changes panes; arrows or `j/k` select rows. Selection previews details. `/` filters the focused list; `Esc` clears the filter or closes an overlay.
- **Live updates:** Preserve selection and scroll position during refreshes. If a selected pod disappears, keep its details visible with a **“Pod terminated”** notice until another pod is selected.

Below about **110 columns**, show namespaces in a drawer. Below **80 columns**, switch between the pod list and full-width details with `Enter` and `Esc`. Always show the current context and namespace to prevent confusion across clusters.
