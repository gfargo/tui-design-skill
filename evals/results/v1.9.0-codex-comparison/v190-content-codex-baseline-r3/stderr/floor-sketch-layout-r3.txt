I'm building a TUI for browsing Kubernetes pods: a namespace sidebar, a pod list, and a detail pane with logs. Design the layout for me.

codex
Use a three-pane layout: namespaces on the left, pods in the middle, and pod details with logs on the right. Keep the current cluster and context visible at all times.

Allocate roughly **18% / 38% / 44%** of the width, with minimum widths of 20, 40, and 48 characters. Let users resize panes. Below about 110 columns, show namespaces as a drawer and switch between the pod list and details.

- **Selection and focus:** Preserve the selected pod as data refreshes. Use a background highlight for selection and an accented pane title or border for keyboard focus. Selecting a namespace filters pods; selecting a pod updates its details.
- **Pod list:** Keep name, readiness, status, and age visible. Add restarts when space allows. Use text and symbols alongside status colors so failures remain legible without color.
- **Details:** Keep metadata compact so logs get most of the height. Provide **Logs / Describe / Events** tabs and a container selector for multi-container pods.
- **Logs:** Follow by default. Scrolling upward pauses following and shows “Paused”; `f` resumes. Support search, timestamps, and horizontal scrolling without wrapping by default.
- **Keyboard:** `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` cycle panes; arrows or `j/k` navigate; `/` filters the focused list or searches logs; `Esc` clears a filter or closes a drawer; `Enter` focuses details.
- **Live updates:** Show loading, disconnected, empty, and permission-denied states inside the affected pane. Preserve existing content during reconnects and display the last successful update time.

Keep destructive actions out of the persistent footer; place them in an explicit action menu with confirmation.
