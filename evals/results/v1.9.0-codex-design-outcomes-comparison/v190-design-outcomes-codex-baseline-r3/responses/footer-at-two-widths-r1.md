**80 columns**
```text
Enter open  / filter  j/k move  r refresh  d delete  y copy path  ? help  q quit
```

**40 columns**
```text
↵ open / filter j/k move ? help q quit
```

The lines use 80 and 38 columns, respectively; `↵` means Enter.

At 40, drop **refresh (20%)**, **delete (8%)**, and **copy path (6%)** to preserve the three most-used actions plus help and quit for discoverability and exit.

**Edit, sort, tag, and command** are omitted at both widths because they have the lowest recorded usage.
